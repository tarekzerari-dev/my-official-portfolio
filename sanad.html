01 — Overview

Digitizing Quranic education management

Sanad is an educational tracking platform designed for Quranic schools and circles. It replaces paper diary logs with a modern digital system for tracking memorization progress (Hifz), daily revision, attendance, and student rankings.

Product Focus Arabic-first educational management & gamified progress tracking
My Role Full-Stack Developer — hybrid persistence, sync layer, hash-routed SPA, Arabic RTL interface, admin and public views
02 — The Problem

Manual paper logs & disconnected parents

Quranic schools traditionally rely on paper notebooks to log students' daily recitation and attendance. This manual approach creates several pain points:

Lost or damaged paper logs

Paper diaries get damaged or lost, losing months of progress history.

Lack of parent visibility

Parents rarely see daily progress or attendance notes until end of term.

03 — Users

Connecting teachers, students, & administration

Sanad serves three primary user groups:

Quran Teachers
Needs a fast way to log daily recitation, revision, and attendance in seconds.
Students & Parents
Monitors daily diary entries, memorization goals, and class leaderboards.
School Administration
Requires a comprehensive overview of all classes, student rosters, and teacher activity.
04 — Design Assumptions

Key UX assumptions

Digital reflection of physical diary

Translating the familiar physical "المفكرة" format into UI reduces learning friction for traditional teachers.

Gamification boosts motivation

Public class leaderboards and achievement points encourage healthy competition and consistent attendance.

05 — RTL & Cultural UX

Arabic-first layout & ergonomics

Sanad was crafted specifically for Right-to-Left (RTL) reading flow and Arabic typographic hierarchy. Surah names, verse range selectors, and grade badges follow natural Arabic reading patterns, ensuring traditional teachers and young students experience zero cognitive friction.

06 — Visual Identity

Connecting tradition with modern UI

The visual identity balances spiritual warmth with clean digital utility.

Growth Palette

Islamic Green (#2D8A4E) as the primary brand color, paired with soft grays and crisp whites to preserve visual clarity.

Arabic Typography

Cairo and Noto Kufi Arabic fonts optimized for legible reading of Quranic terms and UI elements.

Clean Layouts

Card-based structures with subtle borders separate daily logs, student details, and rankings.

Iconography

Soft rounded icons to ensure intuitive navigation for users of all technical backgrounds.

Engineering

Lightweight, offline-tolerant, bilingual

Sanad is a hash-routed single-page application built with Vite and vanilla JavaScript modules — no framework and no heavy UI dependency. That keeps the bundle small enough for school devices while still shipping the full admin and public experience.

Hybrid persistence

Structured records live in Supabase Postgres; uploaded media is stored in Netlify Blobs, so the database stays lean and files stay cheap.

Debounced sync

Writes land in localStorage first, then a debounced Netlify Function pushes them to the cloud, keeping the interface instant on weak connections.

Role-aware SPA

One hash router serves public pages and the authenticated admin panel, with attendance, memorization, points, competitions and leaderboards.

Arabic RTL end to end

Direction, spacing, icon mirroring and typography are handled at the layout level, so the Arabic interface is the primary experience.

Key learnings

Designing for traditional educational settings requires respecting established offline habits while removing manual friction.
