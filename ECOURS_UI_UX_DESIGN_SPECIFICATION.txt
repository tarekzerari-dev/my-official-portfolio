1. EXECUTIVE PRODUCT VISION & CORE UX PHILOSOPHY
================================================================================

1.1 The Problem Space
In the private tutoring and private academy sector, administrative operations 
have historically relied on manual notebooks, fragmented messaging apps, and 
unstructured cash logging. This operational fragmentation causes:
- Severe attendance tracking delays and lost instructional time (5-10 min/session).
- Financial leakage, untracked student debts, and awkward manual payment collection.
- Zero structured visibility for parents regarding student punctuality.
- Lack of consolidated operational data for educational center directors.

1.2 The eCours Product Vision
eCours is engineered as a unified, high-performance, and visually captivating 
educational management SaaS. It turns chaotic administrative tasks into a frictionless, 
delightful, and real-time experience through:
- Ultra-fast QR-based attendance tracking (under 1.5 seconds per student).
- Comprehensive multi-tiered hierarchy (Superadmin -> School Director -> Teacher -> Student).
- Actionable financial telemetry and automated debt tracking.
- A premium, calming, and focused visual aesthetic tailored for high-frequency daily use.

1.3 Design Pillars
- Cognitive Calm: Eliminating visual clutter; presenting complex data in scannable, bite-sized widgets.
- Frictionless Speed: One-click primary actions, instant optimistic UI updates, and zero unnecessary page loads.
- Native RTL-First Polish: Built from the ground up for Arabic typography, natural reading flow, and cultural ergonomics.
- Multi-Role Fluidity: Seamless adaptation whether used by an independent teacher on a smartphone or a school manager on an ultra-wide desktop.

2. PERSONA ARCHITECTURE & USER JOURNEY MAPPING
================================================================================

eCours supports four distinct personas, each with tailored mental models:

2.1 Persona A: The Independent Educator (الأستاذ المستقل)
- Context: Teaches in private or rented classrooms, directly manages students, schedules, and collections.
- Primary Goal: Track attendance instantly at the classroom door and know exactly who paid and who owes money.
- Key UX Surface: Mobile/Tablet Dashboard, QR Scanner, Cohort Group Manager, Instant Student Payment Drawer.

2.2 Persona B: The School / Center Director (مدير المؤسسة / المركز التعليمي)
- Context: Oversees multiple affiliated teachers, shared physical classrooms, hundreds of enrolled students, and center revenues.
- Primary Goal: Macro-management of school staff, classroom capacity, and central tuition fee collection.
- Key UX Surface: School Performance Telemetry, Teacher Registry & Fee Splits, Centralized Student Database, School Attendance Audits.

2.3 Persona C: The Super Administrator (المشرف العام على المنصة)
- Context: Platform operator overseeing multiple schools, independent teachers, overall SaaS revenue, and onboarding links.
- Primary Goal: System health, organization onboarding, subscription link distribution, and global analytics.
- Key UX Surface: Super Admin Control Tower, School Multi-Tenancy Hub, Teacher Profiles, Global Billing Matrix.

2.4 Persona D: The Parent & Student (ولي الأمر والطالب)
- Context: Physical QR card holder and automated notification receiver.
- Primary Goal: Painless check-in, transparent session attendance record, and immediate clarity on monthly fees.
- Key UX Surface: Printable/Mobile QR Identification Card, Absence Notification Triggers.

3. VISUAL DESIGN SYSTEM & BRAND IDENTITY
================================================================================

3.1 Color Palette & Emotional Tone
The color architecture blends educational focus, trust, and modern fintech reliability.

- Primary Brand Spectrum (Educational Emerald & Sage):
  • Emerald-50  (#ecfdf5): Soft background tint for active cards and badges.
  • Emerald-100 (#d1fae5): Subtle borders and inactive container fills.
  • Emerald-500 (#10b981): Primary accent, active indicators, and success highlights.
  • Emerald-600 (#059669): Primary interactive button state and high-contrast call-to-actions.
  • Emerald-700 (#047857): Hover states and emphasized text accents.
  • Emerald-950 (#022c22): Deep background contrast and dark brand grounding.

- Neutral & Structural Spectrum (Slate & Zinc):
  • Slate-50  (#f8fafc): Clean canvas background in Light Mode.
  • Slate-100 (#f1f5f9): Secondary surface fills and divider lines.
  • Slate-200 (#e2e8f0): Card borders and structural framing.
  • Slate-500 (#64748b): Subtitles, helper text, and secondary metadata.
  • Slate-900 (#0f172a): High-contrast primary headings and data numbers.
  • Slate-950 (#020617): Deep space base canvas in Dark Mode.

- Functional & Semantic Spectrum:
  • Emerald / Mint: Present / Paid / Active / Low-risk.
  • Amber / Gold: Late / Pending Payment / Partial Debt / Attention required.
  • Rose / Coral: Absent / Overdue Debt / Critical Action / Deletion.
  • Indigo / Violet: Independent Educator Cohorts / Specialized Subjects.

3.2 Typography System (Bilingual Fluid Scale)
- Primary Arabic Font: "Cairo" — Chosen for its geometric clarity, modern warmth, and exceptional legibility across both dense Arabic numerals and bold Latin codes.
- Primary Latin Font: "Inter" — Clean, neutral, high-legibility sans-serif for numbers, timestamps, and codes.
- Fluid Scaling Equation (CSS Clamp):
  • Heading 1 (Page Title)   : clamp(1.875rem, 1.6rem + 1.375vw, 2.25rem) — Bold 800
  • Heading 2 (Card Header)  : clamp(1.25rem, 1.1rem + 0.75vw, 1.5rem)   — Bold 700
  • Body Text (Data / Labels): clamp(0.875rem, 0.8rem + 0.375vw, 1rem)   — Regular 400 & Medium 500
  • Micro / Metadata         : clamp(0.75rem, 0.7rem + 0.25vw, 0.875rem) — Semi-Bold 600

3.3 Depth, Elevation & Materiality (Glassmorphism + Organic Shadows)
- Glass Panels: `bg-white/80 backdrop-blur-xl border border-emerald-100/60 shadow-float`
- Organic Shadows:
  • Ambient Float : `0 10px 30px -10px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.08)`
  • Hover Lift    : `0 16px 40px -10px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.16)`
  • Emerald Glow  : `0 0 18px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.22)`
- Contextual Watermark Doodles: Subtle SVG illustrations layered seamlessly in card corners (Coin for Finance, People for Groups, Grad Cap for Teachers).

3.4 Dark Mode Architecture
Dark mode is treated as a first-class citizen using `[data-theme="dark"]`. Instead of harsh pure blacks, it leverages deep velvety slate tones (`#020617` and `#0f172a`) with subdued emerald glows, ensuring zero eye-strain during evening tutoring sessions.

4. INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE & NAVIGATION ECOSYSTEM
================================================================================

4.1 Navigation Model
The platform adopts a dual-tier navigation system:
1. Fixed Collapsible Glass Sidebar: Houses primary macro destinations with active status pills and badge counters.
2. Sticky Glass Header: Displays user identity, role badge, instant theme toggle, language switcher, and rapid action triggers (e.g., Quick Scan, Add Student).

4.2 Dual-View Dynamic Routing
- Super Admin Route (`/admin`):
  • Overview (/admin)
  • Schools & Directors (/admin/schools)
  • Teacher Roster (/admin/teachers)
  • Cohorts & Groups (/admin/groups)
  • Master Student Registry (/admin/students)
  • Platform Billing Matrix (/admin/billing)
  • System Settings (/admin/settings)

- Teacher / School Dashboard Route (`/dashboard`):
  • Overview Telemetry (/dashboard)
  • Groups & Cohorts (/groups)
  • Enrolled Students (/students)
  • Live Session Attendance & Logs (/sessions)
  • Financial Tracking & Payments (/finances)
  • Parent Notifications Hub (/notifications)
  • Quick QR Scanner Viewport (/scan)

5. DEEP-DIVE: CORE INTERFACE MODULES & INTERACTION DESIGN
================================================================================

5.1 KPI Telemetry & Stat Cards
- Layout: 6-column responsive grid featuring fluid KPI cards.
- Content Anatomy:
  1. Semantic Icon Container with gentle tinted background.
  2. Bold Arabic Metric Value (formatted with dynamic thousand separators).
  3. Metric Label in Slate-500.
  4. Visual Trend Indicator (percentage increase / badge state).
  5. Corner Watermark Doodle reinforcing categorical context.

5.2 Data Tables & Roster Lists
- Visual Hierarchy:
  • Sticky high-contrast header row with subtle borders.
  • Alternating hover-lift interactions on data rows (`hover:bg-emerald-50/40`).
  • Avatars with localized initials and status indicator dots.
  • Inline badges for educational levels (1AS, 2AS, 3AS - Bac, 4AM - BEM).
- Search & Filter Controls:
  • Instant client-side search bar with keyboard shortcuts.
  • Filter chips for Quick Filtering (By Subject, By School, By Payment Status).

5.3 Modals, Slide-Overs & Creation Flows
- Layering: Semi-transparent backdrop blur (`bg-slate-950/40 backdrop-blur-sm`).
- Motion: Zoom-in (`scale(0.95) -> scale(1.0)`) with smooth cubic bezier curves.
- Input Design: Large touch-friendly input fields with icon adornments, clean RTL placeholder alignments, and active focus rings (`ring-emerald-500/20`).

6. QR CODE SCANNER & REAL-TIME ATTENDANCE EXPERIENCE
================================================================================

6.1 Interaction Flow
1. The teacher or administrative assistant opens `/scan` on their device.
2. Real-time video viewfinder initializes with a holographic scanning frame and animated guide line.
3. As the student presents their physical or digital card, the QR is parsed instantaneously (< 150ms).
4. Audio-Visual Feedback Loop:
   • Instant haptic pulse / crisp chime.
   • The viewfinder border flashes vivid Emerald.
   • Slide-up Student Card appears showing: Name, Photo, Group, and Current Month Payment Status (PAID / UNPAID).
5. Automatic batch logging: The student is marked "Present" in the active session without requiring secondary button confirmations.

6.2 Offline Resilience & Graceful Fallbacks
- Manual Search Fallback: If a student forgets their QR card, an instant live-search input above the viewfinder allows one-tap manual check-in.
- Session Statistics Header: Displays a live circular progress meter: "Present: 18/20 (90%)".

7. FINANCIAL TELEMETRY, DEBT SURVEILLANCE & BILLING UX
================================================================================

7.1 Debt Visual Language
- Clean Debt Indicators: Students with pending payments for the current billing cycle are tagged with an Amber pill ("غير مدفوع") or Rose badge ("متأخر").
- Quick Payment Drawer: Clicking on an unpaid student opens a modal with pre-filled monthly pricing, payment method selector (Cash / Transfer), and one-click submission.

7.2 Revenue Analytics
- Monthly Revenue Chart: Visual breakdown of cash collections vs. outstanding receivables.
- School vs. Independent Teacher Share: For educational centers, financial splits between the school facility fee and teacher remuneration are calculated automatically.

================================================================================
8. MICRO-INTERACTIONS, MOTION PHYSICS & COGNITIVE FEEDBACK LOOPS
================================================================================

8.1 Motion Choreography
- Hover Elevate: Elements subtly rise by 2px (`hover:-translate-y-0.5`) with shadow extension to signal clickability.
- Active Feedback: Buttons scale down to `scale(0.97)` on active click for tactile responsiveness.
- Staggered Entrance: Data tables and dashboard cards animate in sequentially using staggered opacity and translateY keyframes (`@keyframes fadeInUp`).

8.2 Optimistic Updates & Notification Toasts
- TanStack Query Integration: When marking attendance or logging a payment, the UI updates instantly before the network handshake completes.
- Non-Intrusive Toasts: Confirmation messages anchor subtly in the top-start corner, auto-dismissing after 3.5 seconds.

================================================================================
9. LOCALIZATION & RTL-FIRST ARCHITECTURAL MECHANICS
================================================================================

9.1 Native RTL Ergonomics
- Structural Mirroring: Sidebars anchor to the right; progress bars fill from right to left; numerical icons and chevrons properly reflect Arabic reading flow.
- Typography Metrics: Carefully adjusted line-heights (`leading-relaxed`) to prevent Arabic diacritics and glyph ascenders/descenders from clipping.
- Currency & Numerical Formatting: Prices rendered with Algerian Dinar formatting (`دج` / `DZD`) with localized commas.

9.2 Instant Language Switching
- Language context (`Arabic` / `English` / `French`) switches instantaneously without full page reload.

================================================================================
10. ACCESSIBILITY (A11Y), ERGONOMICS & RESPONSIVE BEHAVIOR
================================================================================

10.1 Touch Ergonomics (Mobile-First Dashboard)
- Touch Targets: Every button, input, and interactive pill maintains a minimum 44x44px bounding box.
- Bottom Bar on Mobile: On narrow mobile screens (< 640px), critical actions (Scanner, Groups, Payments) collapse into a thumb-accessible bottom navigation bar.

10.2 Contrast Ratios & Visual Compliance
- All body text against light and dark surfaces meets or exceeds WCAG 2.1 AAA standards (contrast ratio >= 7:1).
- Non-reliant on color alone: Every status indicator pairs color with descriptive typography or iconography (e.g., Checkmark for Present, X for Absent).

11. DESIGN SCALABILITY, FUTURE ROADMAP & STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS
================================================================================

11.1 Progressive Web App (PWA) Offline-First Scanner
- Implement service worker caching and background synchronization so tutors in basement classrooms with zero mobile signal can scan QR codes offline and sync automatically upon reconnection.

11.2 Automated WhatsApp / SMS Gateway Trigger
- Integrate direct WhatsApp API triggers in the Parent Hub allowing teachers to send pre-formatted attendance alerts ("Your son Youssef has checked in at 14:02") with a single tap.

11.3 Automated PDF Student ID Card Batch Generator
- Provide an automated printable template grid (8 cards per A4 page) with high-resolution vector QR codes, school logos, and student photos ready for physical laminating.
